Data leakage

An earlier version of this model reported 100% accuracy — a red flag, not a win. It leaked the outcome through call duration, which isn't known until after a call happens. Removing it and evaluating on a proper held-out split drops accuracy to an honest ~83–90%, with an AUC of 0.76–0.78 — lower, but real, and actually usable for deciding who to call.

02 — Model comparison

Accuracy isn't the metric that matters here

Random Forest looks more "accurate" but misses most real subscribers. Logistic regression trades some precision for catching 3x more of them — the better trade for a bank trying to grow deposits.
